Every animal deserves a second chance

Paws & Hearts is a Johannesburg-based animal rescue that finds loving homes for abandoned and injured cats and dogs. Every animal in our care is vaccinated, sterilised, and ready to become part of a family.

Why Adopt With Us

Every animal is vet-checked, vaccinated, and sterilised before adoption. We also offer free post-adoption advice for the first month, so you're never left figuring things out alone.
