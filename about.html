About Paws & Hearts

Our History

Paws & Hearts started in 2021 as a small foster network run out of a few volunteers' homes in Johannesburg. As more animals needed help than our homes could hold, we opened a small shelter facility that now cares for 30 to 40 animals at any given time.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned and injured animals across Johannesburg.

Vision: A community where no healthy, treatable animal is turned away for lack of a home.

How We Work

Every animal that comes into our care is vet-checked, vaccinated, and sterilised before being listed for adoption. Our small team of staff and volunteers handles daily feeding, walks, and medical care until each animal finds a permanent home.
