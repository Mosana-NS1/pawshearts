Our story

People helping animals find their way home.

Paws & Hearts is a Johannesburg-based animal rescue built on compassion, responsibility and the belief that every healthy, treatable animal deserves a second chance.

Where we started

Our history

Paws & Hearts started in 2021 as a small foster network run from a few volunteers' homes in Johannesburg.

As more animals needed help than our homes could hold, we opened a small shelter facility. Today, the organisation cares for approximately 30 to 40 animals at any given time.

01

Our mission

To rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned and injured animals across Johannesburg.

02

Our vision

A community where no healthy, treatable animal is turned away for lack of a home.

Care with purpose

How we work

Our process is designed to protect the wellbeing of every animal while preparing them for a successful future with their new family.

1

Rescue

Animals are brought into our care from unsafe, abandoned or neglectful situations.

2

Rehabilitate

Each animal receives veterinary attention, vaccinations, sterilisation and daily care.

3

Rehome

We match animals with suitable families and provide guidance throughout the adoption process.
